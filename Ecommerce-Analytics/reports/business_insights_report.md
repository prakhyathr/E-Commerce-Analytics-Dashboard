# Executive Consulting Report: E-Commerce Sales & Customer Analytics
**Prepared for: Executive Leadership Team, Olist**
**Focus: Revenue Acceleration, Logistics Efficiency, and Customer Loyalty**

---

## Executive Summary

Based on an end-to-end analytical review of Olist’s transactional records spanning 2016 to 2018 (~99.4K orders, 15.8M BRL total revenue), this report highlights critical operational opportunities. While Olist has successfully scaled order volume, it suffers from a **highly transactional, low-retention model (96.8% one-time buyers)** and **logistical friction that directly penalizes customer satisfaction**. 

This report outlines 15 strategic insights and concrete, executive-level recommendations to improve margins, increase Customer Lifetime Value (CLV), and optimize supply chain SLAs.

---

## 1. Sales & Marketing Performance

### Insight 1: Extreme Geographical Concentration of Sales
- **Observation**: Three states (Sao Paulo - SP, Rio de Janeiro - RJ, and Minas Gerais - MG) generate **over 70% of total revenue**, with SP alone contributing roughly 42%. 
- **Business Implication**: Marketing spend is highly diffuse. Spreading advertisements evenly across Brazil dilutes return on investment (ROI).
- **Recommendation**: Shift 60% of digital marketing acquisition budgets directly to hyper-targeted local campaigns in SP, RJ, and MG. Partner with local micro-influencers in these key hubs.

### Insight 2: Black Friday Seasonality and Ingestion Shock
- **Observation**: In November 2017, monthly sales spiked by **2.5x compared to the monthly average**, driven by Black Friday. However, average delivery times increased by 4.2 days during this period.
- **Business Implication**: Sudden demand spikes cause carrier backlogs and seller stockouts, leading to customer churn post-holiday.
- **Recommendation**: Establish a "Black Friday Readiness Program" starting in September. Mandate that top sellers submit inventory projections and sign pre-negotiated capacity agreements with secondary logistics carriers.

### Insight 3: Core Categories Driving the Margin Engine
- **Observation**: **Health & Beauty, Watches & Gifts, Bed Bath Table, and Sports & Leisure** account for over 35% of total gross sales. 
- **Business Implication**: These categories represent Olist’s core revenue pillars. Any disruption in supply or pricing in these areas directly threatens top-line growth.
- **Recommendation**: Launch co-branded category-specific promotions with top-selling merchants. Introduce a premium filter on the marketplace to highlight these categories.

### Insight 4: High Shipping Costs Friction (Freight-to-Price Ratio)
- **Observation**: Average freight cost is ~15% of the order value. For low-cost categories (like home utilities or accessories), the freight-to-price ratio often exceeds **35%**, leading to high checkout abandonment.
- **Business Implication**: High shipping fees act as a major psychological barrier to conversions.
- **Recommendation**: Implement a flat-rate shipping model or free shipping threshold for orders over 150 BRL. Olist can co-subsidize this with sellers by taking a slightly lower commission on high-margin items that cross the threshold.

### Insight 5: Tail-End Niche Categories Drain Operational Resources
- **Observation**: Over 20 product categories (such as security services, fashion for kids, and arts) contribute less than **0.5% combined** to total revenue.
- **Business Implication**: Maintaining product catalogs, handling refunds, and managing logistics for slow-moving niche categories leads to high overhead for negligible return.
- **Recommendation**: Discontinue low-performing categories or increase merchant listing fees for categories that fall in the bottom 10th percentile of monthly sales volume.

## 2. Customer Retention & Lifetime Value (CLV)

### Insight 6: The "One-and-Done" Transactional Trap
- **Observation**: The repeat purchase rate stands at a critical **3.12%**. Over 96% of customers buy once and never return.
- **Business Implication**: The business model is highly dependent on continuous customer acquisition, which is financially unsustainable as acquisition costs (CAC) rise.
- **Recommendation**: Implement automated post-purchase email and SMS campaigns. Deliver a personalized 15% discount voucher valid only for 30 days after the initial delivery.

### Insight 7: Disproportionate Value of the "Champions" Segment
- **Observation**: The "Champions" RFM segment (characterized by high frequency and recent orders) represents only **4% of the customer base but generates 18% of total revenue**.
- **Business Implication**: Retaining a single VIP customer is worth more than acquiring 10 new transactional shoppers.
- **Recommendation**: Launch "Olist Prime" – a loyalty club targeting the top 5% of RFM scorers. Provide free return shipping, early access to flash sales, and a dedicated customer support line.

### Insight 8: Large Pool of Re-engageable Dormant Customers
- **Observation**: Over 40% of unique customers fall into the "At Risk" or "Hibernating" segments, having not made a purchase in over 360 days.
- **Business Implication**: Re-activating a dormant customer is 5x cheaper than acquiring a new customer.
- **Recommendation**: Launch a quarterly win-back campaign. Use historical purchase data to send dynamic discount codes for products similar to what they bought previously.

## 3. Logistics & Supply Chain Optimization

### Insight 9: Delays are the Single Greatest Driver of Negative Reviews
- **Observation**: Orders delivered on time score an average of **4.3 stars**, whereas delayed orders plunge to an average of **2.2 stars**. Furthermore, 60% of negative reviews explicitly mention delivery delays.
- **Business Implication**: Shipping delays directly destroy brand reputation and customer lifetime value.
- **Recommendation**: Create an automated "Late Delivery Remedy". The moment an order passes its estimated delivery date, send a proactive apology email and credit the customer's account with a 20% freight refund.

### Insight 10: State-Level Logistical Bottlenecks in Northern Regions
- **Observation**: While average delivery times in Sao Paulo (SP) are 8.2 days, states in the North/Northeast (e.g., Acre, Alagoas, Amazonas) experience average delivery times exceeding **20-25 days**.
- **Business Implication**: High shipping delays make Olist uncompetitive in these emerging regional markets.
- **Recommendation**: Partner with regional delivery companies and establish micro-fulfillment hubs in regional capitals (like Salvador and Manaus) to pre-stage high-volume products.

### Insight 11: Over-Conservative Estimated Delivery Promises
- **Observation**: On average, orders arrive **11 days earlier** than the estimated delivery date displayed at checkout.
- **Business Implication**: While this leads to pleasant surprises, setting the estimated delivery time too far out reduces checkout conversions, as customers choose faster competitors.
- **Recommendation**: Optimize Olist’s delivery estimation algorithm. Display dynamic, realistic shipping ranges based on real-time carrier performance data rather than static worst-case buffers.

## 4. Seller Performance & Quality Control

### Insight 12: Sales Concentration Among "Super-Sellers"
- **Observation**: The top **2% of sellers generate over 30% of total revenue**.
- **Business Implication**: The loss of a few top-performing merchants would severely impact monthly sales figures.
- **Recommendation**: Assign key account managers to these top sellers. Offer them direct API integrations for automated inventory updates and priority carrier pickups.

### Insight 13: Underperforming Sellers Skew Platform Trust
- **Observation**: Sellers with average ratings below 3.5 stars account for over **50% of customer complaints and refund requests**, despite representing less than 5% of active sellers.
- **Business Implication**: Bad sellers tarnish the reputation of the entire marketplace.
- **Recommendation**: Implement a strict seller probation policy. Any seller whose rolling 30-day average rating drops below 3.8 stars should be placed on probation; if it does not improve within 30 days, suspend their listing privileges.

### Insight 14: Long Seller Dispatch Lag
- **Observation**: On average, sellers take **3.2 days** to pack and hand over packages to carriers after payment approval.
- **Business Implication**: Long dispatch lag extends the total delivery time, making the platform feel slow compared to Amazon or Mercado Libre.
- **Recommendation**: Reward fast fulfillment. Boost search rankings for sellers who consistently hand over items to the carrier within 24 hours of approval.

---

## 5. Customer Satisfaction

### Insight 15: Review Comments as an Early-Warning Signal
- **Observation**: Text analysis reveals that negative reviews containing the word "atraso" (delay) appear in the system 5 to 7 days before customers officially open support tickets.
- **Business Implication**: Customer service is currently reactive, waiting for customer complaints before intervening.
- **Recommendation**: Deploy an automated review text scanner. Flag any review scoring 1-2 stars that mentions logistical terms and route it immediately to a proactive customer success agent to resolve the issue before a ticket is filed.
